Fascia Blasting in Málaga: what it is and who it is for

By Diana Noris · 10 October 2026 · 4 min read

Fascia is the connective tissue that wraps around and links muscles and other structures in the body. When it loses mobility or is particularly tight, some people notice areas that feel stiffer, tighter or uneven. Fascia Blasting is body work designed to stimulate exactly those areas.

What is Fascia Blasting?

It is not a conventional massage. It is an intense, controlled manual technique that uses specially designed tools, such as the FasciaBlaster®, to work on the fascia progressively and encourage greater mobility of the tissues. At Sculptology it is one of our specialities, and we adapt it to each body.

What can it offer?

It depends on each body, on consistency and on how each person responds. In general, it can help to:

  • Encourage the mobility and flexibility of the tissue.
  • Work on areas of tension and stiffness.
  • Stimulate local circulation and a feeling of lightness.
  • Improve the appearance of the skin and of the irregularities associated with cellulite.
  • Complement a body-contouring programme.

Results may vary from person to person and cannot be guaranteed.

What is a session like?

We start with an assessment, measurements and photos. Then 10 minutes on the vibration plate and a warm-up of the area. Next we work with the tool progressively, and we finish by following up on your progress. We usually recommend around 6 sessions; at your assessment I will suggest a personalised plan.

Does it hurt? Will I bruise?

It is intense work, but the pressure is adapted to your tolerance. Mild bruising can appear in the first sessions; it fades within a few days and usually lessens with each session.

When should you check with us first?

Tell us before booking if you are pregnant, take anticoagulants or have clotting problems, have varicose veins or a history of thrombosis, or have wounds, infections or sunburn in the area. If you have any medical concerns, please consult your doctor.

To find out more, see the treatment page: Fascia Blasting in Málaga. You can also combine it with wood therapy or lymphatic drainage.
