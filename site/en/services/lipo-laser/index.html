What is it?

A low-level laser applied with pads over the area being treated. It is non-invasive and painless, and helps reduce localised fat and reshape the contours. It is usually done over several sessions and combined with other treatments.

Benefits

  • Painless: you relax while it works.
  • Designed for stubborn areas.
  • No downtime.
  • Boosts the results of cavitation and radiofrequency.

Results may vary from person to person.

Frequently asked questions

What does it feel like?

Practically nothing; at most, a slight warmth.

How many sessions?

We work in a series; Diana will propose a plan at your assessment.

Can I combine it with other treatments?

Yes, it is usually paired with cavitation or radiofrequency.

Before you book, talk to us if…

  • You are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • You have a pacemaker.
  • You have active cancer.
  • You take photosensitising medication.

If you have any medical concerns, please consult your doctor.
